# Data Protection Governance — DSGVO Art. 25 Privacy by Design

> **Authority:** This document defines binding data protection rules for all use cases and reports built with ALUCA (Analytics Library of Use Cases).
>
> **Legal basis:** Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (DSGVO/GDPR), Article 25 — Data Protection by Design and by Default; Article 5 — Principles relating to processing; Article 35 — Data Protection Impact Assessment.
>
> **EDPB guidance:** EDPB Guidelines 4/2019 on Article 25 — Data Protection by Design and by Default.

---

## 1. The Principle (Art. 25)

The controller shall implement appropriate technical and organisational measures which are designed to implement the data-protection principles (Art. 5) effectively and to integrate the necessary safeguards into the processing to meet the requirements of the GDPR and protect the rights of data subjects — **both at the time of determination of the means and at the time of processing itself**.

For this framework this means: **data protection decisions must be made at use case design time** (in `UseCase_Bracket.yaml`), not at report deployment time.

---

## 2. When `data_protection` Is Required

Every `UseCase_Bracket.yaml` must include a `data_protection` block. The minimum required fields are:

| Field | Always Required | Required When |
|---|---|---|
| `personal_data` (bool) | ✅ | — |
| `legal_basis` (enum) | ✅ | — |
| `personal_data_categories` (array) | ❌ | `personal_data: true` |
| `data_minimisation` (string) | ✅ recommended | Any use case |
| `retention_period_days` (int) | ❌ | `personal_data: true` |
| `access_restriction` (string) | ✅ recommended | Any use case |
| `dpia_required` (bool) | ✅ recommended | Any use case |
| `ai_generated_output` (bool) | ✅ recommended | Reports with smartNarrativeVisual |
| `ai_grounding_measure` (string) | ❌ | `ai_generated_output: true` |

---

## 3. Personal Data Assessment Guide

A use case processes **personal data** (DSGVO Art. 4(1)) when any of the following applies:

- The detail matrix or drill-through exposes **individual employee** metrics (headcount, performance scores, HR data)
- Customer-level data where **individual customers are natural persons** (B2C — not B2B entities)
- Any field that **indirectly identifies** a natural person (e.g., unique customer ID linkable to a name in another system)
- **Pseudonymised** data still qualifies as personal data if re-identification is reasonably possible

**Not personal data** (for Art. 25 purposes in this framework):
- Aggregated KPIs at business-unit, region, or product-category level
- B2B commercial data at the company/entity level (legal persons, not natural persons)
- Operational metrics at plant, shift, or machine level (absent employee attribution)

## 4. Legal Basis Mapping

| Legal Basis | Code | When applicable |
|---|---|---|
| Not applicable (no personal data) | `not_applicable` | Aggregated B2B or operational data |
| Consent (Art. 6(1)(a)) | `art6_1a_consent` | Opt-in analytics programs |
| Contract performance (Art. 6(1)(b)) | `art6_1b_contract` | Employee performance tracking within employment contract |
| Legal obligation (Art. 6(1)(c)) | `art6_1c_legal_obligation` | Mandatory reporting (tax, regulatory) |
| Public task (Art. 6(1)(e)) | `art6_1e_public_task` | Public sector organisations |
| Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)) | `art6_1f_legitimate_interest` | B2B analytics where LIA has been conducted |

---

## 5. Data Minimisation (Art. 5(1)(c))

Reports must display only the data **adequate, relevant, and limited to what is necessary** in relation to the stated analytical purpose (the decision question in `ux_layout_rules.page_N.decision_question`).

**Checks:**

- Does the detail matrix expose columns that cannot answer the decision question? Remove them.
- Is the granularity of the detail level finer than necessary? Aggregate up.
- Are there drill-through paths that expose raw person-level data? Apply row-level security.

---

## 6. AI-Generated Output and Personal Data

When `ai_generated_output: true`, the narrative text is generated by a machine learning model. Two additional risks apply:

1. **Hallucination risk** — the model may generate factually incorrect statements (Ji et al. 2023, Survey of Hallucination in NLG). Grounding rules in `Storytelling_Principles.md §13` are mandatory.
2. **Data leakage risk** — if personal data is passed to an external LLM API (Azure OpenAI, etc.), the data transfer may constitute a data export requiring adequate safeguards (DSGVO Art. 44–49). For Power BI Smart Narrative: the model processes data locally on the Power BI service (EU data residency applies to Fabric EU tenants).

---

## 7. DPIA Trigger Criteria (Art. 35)

A Data Protection Impact Assessment is **required** (`dpia_required: true`) when the processing is likely to result in a high risk, including:

- **Systematic monitoring** of employees or public areas
- **Large-scale** processing of special-category data (Art. 9)
- **Profiling** that produces legal or similarly significant effects on individuals
- **Innovative technologies** with unknown risks (novel AI integrations)

Consult the Data Protection Officer before setting `dpia_required: false` for employee-level HR analytics.

---

## 8. Reference Implementation

`core/usecases/core/COM-001_Sales_Performance/UseCase_Bracket.yaml` contains the reference `data_protection` block for a non-personal-data aggregated commercial use case.

For an HR or CX use case with personal data, set:

```yaml
data_protection:
  personal_data: true
  personal_data_categories:
    - performance_data
    - contact_data
  legal_basis: art6_1b_contract
  data_minimisation: >
    Employee metrics displayed at team level only; individual attribution
    suppressed for groups < 5 persons (statistical disclosure limitation).
  retention_period_days: 90
  access_restriction: >
    HR business partner and department head only; row-level security
    enforced — employees see only their direct-report aggregates.
  dpia_required: false
  ai_generated_output: false
```

---

## 9. Enforcement

The `data_protection` block is validated by `tooling/preflight.py` via `tooling/generator/schemas/usecase_bracket.schema.json`. Use cases without a `data_protection` block will generate a schema validation warning and must be remediated before production deployment.
