T1 Strategic Overview · Hero layout · Z-pattern reading · Use case: FIN-001 Group Financial Performance
kpi_card_hero line_chart bar_chart_horizontal text_callout
Zone 1 · 3 seconds
Hero KPI card (col 0–4) dominates — largest element, top-left (Z-start). Signal is immediate: RED / BELOW TARGET. Two supporting KPIs fill col 4–8. Strategic signal callout (col 8–12) reinforces with context.
Zone 3 · 30 seconds
Trend chart (col 0–7) shows the quarterly journey — annotated inflection where margin crossed below threshold. Portfolio ranking (col 7–12) shows scope: 3 of 5 BUs are below target.
Big Idea · 30s test
Reader should state: "EBITDA margin is below 15% for 3 consecutive quarters. It's getting worse. 3 of 5 BUs are affected. Strategic intervention required." If not constructable in 30s, the design has failed.

F-pattern reading flow
Top scan: Smart Narrative gives the situation in 1 sentence. Left column: Slicer pane shows drillthrough context (DACH pre-selected). Table: entity name leftmost, delta bars highlight worst accounts.
Action Panel · Right anchor
Action Panel is visible immediately on page load — full height, right side. T4 design principle: recommendation leads, evidence follows. Fields: directive title → WHY → Owner → Due → Impact → Steps → Priority.
Big Idea · 10s test (T4)
Decision owner should answer in 10s: "Cap promo depth in DACH to ≤15%. Owner: Comm Mgr DACH. Due: End October. Impact: +€1.2M GM." If any field requires reading the chart first, the Action Panel has failed.
