# Business Glossary

## Purpose

This glossary defines a **minimal, shared business vocabulary** used across the
Analytics Use Case Library.

It exists to:

- avoid misunderstandings between business and analytics teams
- provide stable semantic grounding for AI / Copilot
- ensure consistent interpretation across domains

This glossary is a **reference**, not a prerequisite.

---

## Core Business Concepts

### Impact Dimension

A strategic lens used to classify KPIs and Use Cases by the **business outcome they influence**  
(e.g. Growth, Profitability, Liquidity, Efficiency).

Each KPI and Use Case references **exactly one** Impact Dimension.

---

### Use Case

A standardized, decision-oriented business question answered with analytics.  
A Use Case always results in a **decision or action**, not just insight.

---

### KPI (Key Performance Indicator)

A quantitative measure used to track performance against a defined business objective.  
KPIs are governed, documented, and reused across reports and Use Cases.

---

### Strategic KPI

A top-level KPI directly linked to company strategy and executive decisions.  
Typically monitored by senior management.

---

### Tactical KPI

A KPI used to steer departments or functions toward strategic targets.  
Acts as a bridge between strategy and operations.

---

### Operational KPI

A day-to-day KPI used to control and optimize operational processes.

---

### Action Code

A standardized, prescriptive description of **what action to take** when a KPI shows a relevant deviation.

Action Codes define **Do / Stop / Shift** actions and make analytics actionable.

---

### Prescriptive Analytics

An analytics stage focused on recommending **concrete actions**, not just explaining results.

In this framework, prescriptive analytics is operationalized via Action Codes.

---

### Reporting Level

Classification of a Use Case based on decision horizon:

- Strategic
- Tactical
- Operational

---

### Analytics Stage

Classification of analytical maturity:

- Descriptive
- Diagnostic
- Predictive
- Prescriptive

---

## Governance Concepts

### Business Owner

Accountable for the **business meaning and interpretation** of a KPI or Use Case.

---

### Data Owner

Responsible for **data quality, lineage, and availability** of the underlying data.

---

### Report Owner

Responsible for usability, clarity, and decision support of a report.

---

### Definition of Done (DoD)

A set of criteria confirming that a KPI or Use Case is complete, validated, and ready for use.

---

## Usage Rules

- Terms in this glossary must be **domain-independent**
- KPI-specific explanations belong in the **KPI Catalog**
- Technical concepts belong in the **Technical Glossary**
- New terms are added **only if reused across multiple domains**
