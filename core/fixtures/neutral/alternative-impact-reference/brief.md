# Synthetic alternative-impact reference

Maintained test fixture for WB-008. Not customer evidence, not a commercial agreement, not a tenant deployment.

Business frame: an invented manufacturer of industrial equipment with series products, customer projects and service. Two detailed domains are in scope: sales (order to cash) and finance (general ledger). The declared source system is SAP S/4HANA; the source objects are standard SAP table names used only as contract labels. No SAP system, SAP demo data, real company or customer is modelled, and no extract rows are included.

Accepted baseline: DEV / TEST / PROD for both domains, released as an immutable revision.

Draft alternative: DEV / PROD. The comparison must show the effects on environment lanes, role demand, effort assumption, lane acceptance criterion, workspace and item manifests, and test obligations. It must leave the accepted baseline revision, its release record and the repository history unchanged.

Named staffing, rates, duration, tenant behavior and customer acceptance are out of scope.
