# Aurora Operating Model

## 1. Operating Philosophy
Aurora operates a **centrally steered, regionally executed** omnichannel model.

* **Group Level:** Strategy, capital allocation, core KPIs, and standards.
* **Regional Level:** Execution, assortment tuning, and local performance steering.
* **Centralized:** Data, technology, and governance to ensure scale and consistency.

The model balances **scale efficiency**, **local market responsiveness**, and **governance stability**.

---

## 2. End-to-End Value Chain
Aurora follows a closed retail value cycle across six key stages:

1. **Plan**
    * *Activities:* Financial Planning, Budgeting, Category Strategy, Demand Forecasting, Inventory Planning.
    * *Focus:* Margin quality, working capital, seasonal demand shaping.
2. **Source**
    * *Activities:* Procurement, Supplier Management, Private Label Development.
    * *Focus:* Supplier reliability, cost negotiation, ESG compliance, sourcing diversification.
3. **Make / Move**
    * *Activities:* Allocation, Distribution Center Operations, Transport, Last Mile.
    * *Focus:* Service level, inventory turns, cost-to-serve, cross-channel inventory balancing.
4. **Sell**
    * *Activities:* Retail Stores, E-Commerce (Web & App), Marketplaces, Wholesale.
    * *Focus:* Conversion, basket size, channel margin, promotion effectiveness.
5. **Serve**
    * *Activities:* Customer Service, Returns, Warranty, After-Sales.
    * *Focus:* Return rate management, NPS, customer lifetime value (CLV), operational cost control.
6. **Govern & Enable**
    * *Activities:* Finance, HR, ESG, IT, Data, Risk & Compliance.
    * *Focus:* Financial control, regulatory compliance, people capability, data & technology enablement.

## 3. Organizational Structure
Aurora utilizes a matrix structure combining Group governance with regional P&L accountability.

### Group Executive Board
* **CEO** – Overall strategy and performance
* **CFO** – Financial steering, capital allocation
* **COO** – Operations & Supply Chain
* **CCO** – Commercial & Category
* **CMO** – Brand & Customer
* **CHRO** – People & Organization
* **CIO** – Technology & Platforms
* **CSO** – ESG & Sustainability
* **CRO** – Risk & Compliance

### Regional & Functional Structure
* **Regions:** Each region (led by a Regional Managing Director) holds P&L responsibility and executes within centrally defined guardrails.
* **Functions:** Cross-regional departments (e.g., Category Management, Pricing, Tech) define the standards and frameworks that regions must follow.

---

## 4. Domain Map (Analytics & Decision Domains)
Domains are aligned with **business accountability**, not departmental org charts.

1. **Commercial:** Revenue, Pricing, Promotion, Margin Steering.
2. **Operational Efficiency:** Inventory, Logistics, Cost-to-Serve, Service Levels.
3. **Customer & Market:** Retention, CLV, Segmentation, Market Share.
4. **Corporate & Strategy:** Financial Performance, Liquidity, Capital Efficiency.
5. **ESG:** Emissions, Energy, Sustainable Sourcing.
6. **Governance:** Risk, Compliance, Controls.
7. **Innovation & People:** Workforce Productivity, Capability, Innovation Portfolio.

---

## 5. KPI Ownership Model
Ownership is defined by **decision accountability**, rather than reporting lines.

| Domain | Executive Owner | Accountable For |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Commercial** | CCO | Revenue, Margin, Promotion ROI |
| **Operational Efficiency** | COO | Service Level, Inventory Turns, Cost-to-Serve |
| **Customer & Market** | CMO | Retention, CLV, Customer Health |
| **Corporate & Strategy** | CFO | Profitability, Cash Flow, Capital Efficiency |
| **ESG** | CSO | Emissions, Energy, Supplier Compliance |
| **Governance** | CRO | Risk Exposure, Compliance KPIs |
| **Innovation & People** | CHRO | Productivity, Talent Metrics |

> **KPI Owners** approve definitions, validate thresholds, and own the action logic. They do *not* own technical implementation.

---

## 6. Accountability Model
Aurora applies a three-layer accountability structure to prevent "orphaned" KPIs:

* **KPI Owner (Executive Level):** Owns the definition and the target.
* **Domain Owner (Senior Business Lead):** Owns use cases and action applicability.
* **Operational Owner (Regional / Functional):** Executes actions and delivers outcomes.

---

## 7. Row-Level Security (RLS) Relevance
Data visibility is strictly aligned with operational responsibility:

| Role | Scope |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Store Manager** | Own Store |
| **Area Manager** | Assigned Store Cluster |
| **Regional Manager** | Entire Region |
| **Logistics Manager** | Assigned DC / Hub |
| **Functional Head** | Functional Scope (e.g., Marketing, Supply Chain) |
| **Executive Board** | Global Visibility |

---

## 8. Steering Logic
Aurora is steered through a cascading hierarchy of **Strategic**, **Domain**, and **Operational KPIs**, supported by **Standardized Action Codes**. This ensures local autonomy while maintaining global consistency and traceable decision logic.
