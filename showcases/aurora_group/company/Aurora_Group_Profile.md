# Aurora Group SE | Corporate Profile

## 1. Company Overview
Aurora Group SE is a European **omnichannel consumer goods and retail company** headquartered in Amsterdam. The company operates an integrated retail ecosystem combining physical stores, e-commerce, marketplaces, wholesale partnerships, and a proprietary logistics network.

* **Positioning:** Mid-to-premium lifestyle retailer.
* **Strengths:** Strong private label penetration and increasing digital revenue share.
* **Growth:** A mix of organic expansion and selective acquisitions over 15 years, resulting in a heterogeneous yet scalable operating model.

## 2. Industry & Competitive Positioning
* **Industry:** Omnichannel Consumer Goods & Retail
* **Market Environment:** Highly competitive and margin-sensitive, characterized by:
  * Strong private label competition and high promotional intensity.
  * Rising customer acquisition costs and supply chain volatility.
  * Increasing ESG (Environmental, Social, and Governance) regulations.
* **Competitive Edge:** Differentiated assortment, data-driven pricing, a strong urban footprint, and a mobile-first e-commerce strategy.

## 3. Geographic Footprint
Aurora operates across five core European regions:
1. **DACH** (Germany, Austria, Switzerland)
2. **Benelux** (Belgium, Netherlands, Luxembourg)
3. **Nordics**
4. **Southern Europe**
5. **Central & Eastern Europe (CEE)**

> **Note:** DACH and Benelux represent the largest revenue markets. Cross-border e-commerce and centralized procurement provide scale, while local pricing adapts to regional demand.

## 4. Sales Channels
Aurora utilizes a fully integrated omnichannel model:

| Channel | Description |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Retail Stores** | ~500 locations in urban and high-frequency areas. |
| **E-Commerce** | Growing double-digit annually; >65% of traffic is mobile. |
| **Marketplaces** | Selective presence to expand reach and manage inventory. |
| **Wholesale & B2B** | Strategic partnerships in selected markets. |

**Integration Features:** Click & collect, ship-from-store, centralized inventory visibility, and unified customer identity.

## 5. Logistics & Supply Chain Network
The group employs a **hybrid logistics structure**:
* **Facilities:** 6 Central Distribution Centers (DCs), 40 Cross-Dock Hubs, and 3 Dedicated E-Commerce Fulfillment Centers.
* **Inbound:** Sea (Asia sourcing), Road (EU), and selective Air (seasonal peaks).
* **Outbound:** DC → Hub → Store OR DC/E-Fulfillment → End Customer.
* **Key Challenges:** Inventory balancing, forecast accuracy, returns management, and last-mile cost pressure.

## 6. Customer Base
* **Scale:** 20 million active customers.
* **Engagement:** Strong loyalty program penetration; shift toward digital-first users.
* **Regional Nuance:** High price sensitivity observed in Southern and CEE regions.
* **Strategic Focus:** Retention, Lifetime Value (LTV), and personalization at scale.

## 7. Workforce & Organization
~15,000 employees distributed across:
* Retail Operations & Logistics
* Commercial, Buying, & Finance
* IT, Digital, Data, & Analytics
* ESG, Compliance, & Corporate Functions

The organization balances **centralized strategic governance** with **regional execution autonomy**.

## 8. Financial Profile (Indicative)
* **Revenue:** Multi-billion EUR annually.
* **Margins:** Mid-single digit EBIT margin.
* **Dynamics:** Strong working capital exposure (inventory intensity) and Q4-weighted seasonality.
* **Steering Focus:** Margin quality, inventory turns, cash conversion cycle, and cost-to-serve optimization.

## 9. ESG & Regulatory Environment
Aurora is navigating increasing EU ESG reporting regulations with a focus on:
* **Carbon Footprint:** Scope 1, 2, and 3 emissions.
* **Operations:** Energy consumption per store and packaging reduction.
* **Supply Chain:** Sustainable sourcing compliance and supplier ESG scoring.

## 10. Digital & Data Maturity
Aurora has transitioned toward a data-centric model through:
* **Infrastructure:** Centralized data platform and standardized semantic layer.
* **Analytics:** Governed KPI catalog and AI-assisted forecasting/pricing pilots.
* **Ongoing Goals:** Aligning cross-domain KPIs and evolving from descriptive reporting to **action-oriented decision steering**.

## 11. Role in the Analytics Framework
Aurora Group serves as the synthetic yet realistic enterprise context for:
* 73 cross-domain Use Cases.
* Strategic KPI alignment and domain ownership structures.
* AI-ready structured artifacts and semantic model blueprints.
