# XD-003 – Business Factsheet

## 1. Summary
- **Business Goal:** Provide a concise, board-ready cockpit of enterprise KPIs with drillable drivers.
- **Target Audience:** CEO, CFO, COO, CCO, CHRO, Board.
- **Business Priority:** High.
- **Expected Impact:** Faster decisions, aligned priorities, clear ownership of KPI gaps.

## 2. Core Questions
- What is the current performance vs Plan and LY across growth, margin, cash, service, people, ESG?
- Where are the largest KPI gaps and which drivers explain them?
- Which actions and owners are assigned to close the gaps?
- How is performance trending over the last 12–24 months?

## 3. KPI Set (Business View)

| KPI Name           | Purpose                      | Business Definition                   | Interpretation                   | Decision Relevance |
|--------------------|------------------------------|---------------------------------------|----------------------------------|--------------------|
| Revenue Growth %   | Top-line momentum            | (Net Sales – LY) / LY                 | Growth health                    | Commercial focus   |
| Gross Margin %     | Profitability quality        | (Net Sales – COGS) / Net Sales        | Margin quality                   | Pricing/cost       |
| EBITDA Margin %    | Operating profitability      | EBITDA / Net Sales                    | Overall profit                   | OpEx discipline    |
| Cash Conversion Cycle| Liquidity speed            | DSO + DIO – DPO                       | Cash efficiency                  | WC actions         |
| Service Level %    | Customer reliability         | Delivered OTIF / Total                | Service health                   | Supply/Logistics   |
| Turnover %         | People stability             | Leavers / Avg Headcount               | Talent risk                      | HR actions         |
| ESG-Aligned Revenue % | Sustainability progress    | ESG revenue / Total revenue           | ESG positioning                  | ESG roadmap        |

## 4. Business Logic & Thresholds
- KPIs flagged red if below target tolerance (e.g., GM % -0.5 pp vs Plan, CCC +5 days, Service <97 %).
- Trend-based alerts if negative for ≥2 periods.
- Ownership and action codes linked per KPI.

## 5. Action Codes (Business Perspective)

| Code | Name                   | Business Description                    | Typical Trigger          | Expected Effect          |
|------|------------------------|-----------------------------------------|--------------------------|--------------------------|
| SP1  | Strategy Alignment     | Cross-KPI prioritization and roadmap    | Multi-KPI gaps           | Focused initiatives      |
| P2   | Pricing/Revenue Guardrails | Improve GM/revenue mix              | GM % decline             | GM uplift                |
| W1   | Working Capital Sprint | Fix CCC via DSO/DIO/DPO                 | CCC slippage             | Shorter CCC              |
| O2   | Process Improvement    | Fix execution gaps (service/quality)    | Service/quality misses   | Higher service quality   |

## 6. 3-30-300 Page Layout

### 6.1 3-Second Layer (Insight)
- KPI cards for top 6–8 enterprise KPIs with Plan/LY deltas.

### 6.2 30-Second Layer (Story)
- Small multiples: trends per KPI.
- Waterfall/bridge for margin or cash where relevant.
- Bar/heatmap for KPI gaps by region/business unit.

### 6.3 300-Second Layer (Detail)
- Matrix: Region/BU with KPI set and variances.
- Drill to driver pages (sales, margin, cash, service, people, ESG).
- Action/owner table with status and due dates.

## 7. Dependencies & Constraints
- Reliable consolidated financials, supply/service, people, and ESG data.
- Clear KPI definitions and targets; owner mapping.
- Consistent regional/BU hierarchies.

## 8. Success Criteria
- Single source of truth for executives.
- KPI gaps identified with owners and actions.
- Faster decision cycles and fewer conflicting reports.
