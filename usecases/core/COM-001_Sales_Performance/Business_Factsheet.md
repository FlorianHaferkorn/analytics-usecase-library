# COM-001 – Business Factsheet

## 1. Summary
- **Business Goal:** Identify and explain deviations in Net Sales vs Plan and Last Year, and quantify the key growth drivers (price, volume, mix, channel, region).
- **Target Audience:** Executive Management (CCO, CFO), Sales Management, Commercial Controlling.
- **Business Priority:** High.
- **Expected Impact:** Earlier detection of negative trends, targeted corrective actions, improved revenue growth and stabilization of gross margin through fact-based steering.

## 2. Core Questions
- Where do Net Sales deviate most vs Plan and vs Last Year?
- What are the main drivers of the Δ Net Sales (price, volume, mix)?
- Which regions, channels or products underperform?
- Which actions in pricing, discounting, demand or inventory close the gaps?

## 3. KPI Set (Business View)

| KPI Name               | Purpose                         | Definition                                                | Interpretation                        | Decision Relevance             |
|------------------------|----------------------------------|------------------------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------|--------------------------------|
| Net Sales Amount       | Revenue performance              | Invoice-level net revenue                                 | ↑ = stronger sales                     | primary steering metric        |
| Revenue Growth %       | Growth vs last year              | (NS − NS LY) / NS LY                                      | trend indicator                        | forecasting & planning         |
| Sales vs Plan %        | Target deviation                 | (NS − Plan) / Plan                                        | >0 = above plan                         | budget adherence               |
| Gross Margin %         | Margin quality                   | (Net Sales − COGS) / Net Sales                            | ↓ indicates leakage                    | pricing & cost control         |
| Price/Volume/Mix Effect| Breakdown of Δ Net Sales         | price effect, volume effect, mix effect                   | identifies dominant driver             | prioritization of actions      |

## 4. Business Logic & Thresholds
- Revenue Growth % < 0 for two periods = critical trend.
- Sales vs Plan % < −5 % = management attention required.
- Gross Margin % significantly below target = pricing/mix issue.
- Price Effect negative & Volume stable = price sensitivity.
- Volume negative & Price positive = demand or channel issue.

## 5. Action Codes (Business Perspective)

| Code | Name                  | Description                               | Trigger                         | Expected Effect           |
|------|-----------------------|-------------------------------------------|----------------------------------|---------------------------|
| P1   | Price Review          | Review and adjust list/net prices         | negative price effect            | stabilize GM %            |
| P2   | Discount Optimization | Reduce or rationalize discounts           | high discount level              | reduce leakage            |
| D1   | Demand Stimulation    | Drive demand in weak regions              | negative volume effect           | increase volume           |
| I1   | Inventory Rebalancing | Reallocate inventory                       | recurring out-of-stocks          | reduce sales loss         |

## 6. 3–30–300 Page Layout

### **6.1 3-Second Layer**
- KPI Cards:
  - Net Sales Amount  
  - Revenue Growth %  
  - Sales vs Plan %  
  - Gross Margin %  
  - Driver summary (P/V/M)

### **6.2 30-Second Layer**
- Ranking: Region / Channel / Product (horizontal bar)
- Trend: Net Sales + Growth (12–24 months)
- Composition: Price/Volume/Mix Waterfall

### **6.3 300-Second Layer**
- Drilldown: Region → Country → Customer
- Product & Category breakdown
- Downloadable detail table (Excel/CSV)

## 7. Dependencies & Constraints
- Plan values required for variance analysis
- Stable organizational and product hierarchies needed
- No meaningful interpretation without consistent COGS logic
- Promotions optional but recommended for better price understanding

## 8. Success Criteria
- Consistent KPI definitions across Sales & Controlling
- ≥80 % adoption in monthly sales reviews
- Documented and repeatable actions based on deviations
