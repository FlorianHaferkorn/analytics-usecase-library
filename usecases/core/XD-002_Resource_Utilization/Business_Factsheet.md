# XD-002 – Business Factsheet

## 1. Summary
- **Business Goal:** Maximize resource utilization (people, equipment) while maintaining quality and service.
- **Target Audience:** COO, Operations, Workforce Planning, Finance.
- **Business Priority:** Medium/High.
- **Expected Impact:** +2–5 pp utilization, lower overtime/idle time, better cost-to-serve.

## 2. Core Questions
- Which sites/teams/assets have low utilization or high idle time?
- How do schedule adherence, absenteeism, and overtime affect productivity?
- Where do bottlenecks or imbalances create under/over-utilization?
- Which actions (shifts, cross-skilling, reallocation) yield fastest improvement?

## 3. KPI Set (Business View)

| KPI Name                | Purpose                         | Business Definition                         | Interpretation                     | Decision Relevance |
|-------------------------|---------------------------------|---------------------------------------------|------------------------------------|--------------------|
| Utilization %           | Resource usage                  | Productive Time / Available Time            | Core efficiency indicator          | Staffing/load mix  |
| Overtime %              | Cost/strain indicator           | Overtime Hours / Total Hours                | High = cost/risk                   | Schedule changes   |
| Idle Time %             | Waste indicator                 | Idle Hours / Available Hours                | High = imbalance                   | Load rebalancing   |
| Throughput per Hour     | Productivity                    | Output Units / Productive Hour              | Output efficiency                  | Process/skill focus|
| Schedule Adherence %    | Plan discipline                 | Planned vs actual staffed hours             | Low = planning or attendance issue | Planning accuracy  |

## 4. Business Logic & Thresholds
- Utilization < 85 % or trending down = re-balance load.
- Overtime > 10 % with low utilization elsewhere = reassign resources.
- Idle Time > 10 % = scheduling/process issue.
- Schedule Adherence < 95 % = address absenteeism/planning.

## 5. Action Codes (Business Perspective)

| Code | Name                          | Business Description                     | Typical Trigger              | Expected Effect         |
|------|-------------------------------|------------------------------------------|------------------------------|-------------------------|
| L2   | Productivity Boost            | Cross-skill, SMED, reduce micro-stops    | Low utilization/productivity | Higher throughput       |
| O2   | Process Improvement           | Fix scheduling/assignment processes      | High idle or overtime        | Better balance          |
| PC4  | Capacity Balancing            | Shift load across teams/sites            | Local overload/underuse      | Stable utilization      |
| H1   | Attendance/Shift Discipline   | Improve adherence, absence management    | Low schedule adherence       | Higher availability     |

## 6. 3-30-300 Page Layout

### 6.1 3-Second Layer (Insight)
- KPI cards: Utilization %, Overtime %, Idle Time %, Throughput per Hour, Schedule Adherence %.

### 6.2 30-Second Layer (Story)
- Bar: Utilization by site/team/asset.
- Line: Utilization and Overtime trend.
- Pareto: Idle time reasons.

### 6.3 300-Second Layer (Detail)
- Matrix: Site → Team/Asset with utilization, overtime, idle time, throughput.
- Drill: shift/day detail, reason codes.
- Export rebalancing/cross-skilling actions.

## 7. Dependencies & Constraints
- Accurate time tracking (productive/idle/overtime).
- Assignment and schedule plan data.
- Output measures aligned with teams/assets.

## 8. Success Criteria
- Utilization improving to target with lower overtime.
- Reduced idle time and balanced load across sites/teams.
- Factsheet used in weekly workforce/ops reviews.
