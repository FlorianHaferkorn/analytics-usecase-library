# FIN-002 – Business Factsheet

## 1. Summary
- **Business Goal:** Improve operating profit by reducing unit cost and controlling OpEx vs Plan.
- **Target Audience:** CFO, Operations/Plant Managers, Procurement, Finance Controlling.
- **Business Priority:** High.
- **Expected Impact:** -2–4 % unit cost, better OpEx discipline, higher GM/EBITDA.

## 2. Core Questions
- Which plants/lines/products have the highest unit cost and why?
- Where does OpEx deviate vs Plan and LY?
- Which suppliers, materials, or processes drive cost variance?
- Which improvement actions will move GM/EBITDA fastest?

## 3. KPI Set (Business View)

| KPI Name             | Purpose                       | Business Definition                          | Interpretation                       | Decision Relevance |
|----------------------|-------------------------------|----------------------------------------------|--------------------------------------|--------------------|
| Unit Cost            | Cost efficiency               | Total Cost / Units produced or sold          | Lower = more efficient               | Focus lines/plants |
| COGS % of Sales      | Margin quality                | COGS / Net Sales                             | Higher = margin pressure             | Pricing/procurement|
| OpEx vs Plan %       | Spend control                 | (OpEx – Plan) / Plan                         | Gap to plan                          | Budget discipline  |
| Material Cost %      | Direct material pressure      | Material Cost / Net Sales                    | Key lever for GM                     | Supplier actions   |
| Labor Productivity % | Workforce efficiency          | Output / Labor Hours or Cost                 | Lower = utilization issue            | Staffing, training |

## 4. Business Logic & Thresholds
- Unit Cost > Plan by >3 % for 2 periods = action required.
- COGS % of Sales up >1 pp with flat volume = price or procurement issue.
- OpEx vs Plan % > +5 % = discretionary spend review.
- Material Cost % rise >2 pp = supplier/BOM renegotiation.

## 5. Action Codes (Business Perspective)

| Code | Name                      | Business Description                        | Typical Trigger                 | Expected Effect       |
|------|---------------------------|---------------------------------------------|---------------------------------|-----------------------|
| PC2  | Cost Out / Re-Negotiate   | Revisit supplier terms, BOM, logistics      | Material Cost % rising          | -2–4 % unit cost      |
| O2   | Process Improvement       | Lean/Six Sigma to remove waste              | Unit Cost > Plan, low OEE       | -1–3 % unit cost      |
| M1   | Make/Buy Optimization     | Shift to optimal sourcing mix               | Capacity/cost imbalance         | Lower conversion cost |
| L2   | Productivity Boost        | Labor productivity programs                 | Labor Productivity decline      | +3–8 % productivity   |

## 6. 3-30-300 Page Layout

### 6.1 3-Second Layer (Insight)
- KPI cards: Unit Cost, COGS % of Sales, OpEx vs Plan %, Material Cost %, Labor Productivity %.

### 6.2 30-Second Layer (Story)
- Waterfall: Cost variance vs Plan (material, labor, energy, overhead).
- Bar: Unit Cost by plant/line/product.
- Line: OpEx vs Plan trend (12–24M).

### 6.3 300-Second Layer (Detail)
- Matrix: Plant → Line → Product with Unit Cost, Material %, Labor %, Overhead.
- Drill to supplier/material contribution.
- Exportable action list per plant/owner.

## 7. Dependencies & Constraints
- Reliable BOM and routing data; consistent UoM.
- Standard cost vs actual cost availability.
- OpEx classification aligned with Plan.

## 8. Success Criteria
- Sustained reduction in Unit Cost and COGS %.
- OpEx within plan tolerance (<2–3 % variance).
- Productivity trend improving with stable quality.
- Adoption in monthly cost/performance reviews.
