# COM-002 – Business Factsheet

## 1. Summary
- **Business Goal:** Detect and reduce margin leakage by analyzing Gross Margin %, price realization, discount levels and mix effects across regions, channels and products.
- **Target Audience:** CCO, CFO, Commercial Excellence, Pricing, Controlling.
- **Business Priority:** High (direct impact on profitability).
- **Expected Impact:** Improved Gross Margin %, optimized discount structure, reduction of uncontrolled giveaways, more consistent pricing discipline.

## 2. Core Questions
- Where do we lose margin vs Plan and vs Last Year?
- Which regions/channels/products show unusually low GM %?
- How far do effective net prices deviate from list price logic?
- Are discounts justified by volume uplift or strategic value?
- What drives margin leakage (price, discount, mix, cost)?
- Which pricing or mix adjustments close the profitability gap?

## 3. KPI Set (Business View)

| KPI Name                 | Purpose                                | Definition                                                          | Interpretation                         | Decision Relevance             |
|--------------------------|------------------------------------------|----------------------------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------|--------------------------------|
| Gross Margin %           | Profitability quality                    | (Net Sales − COGS) / Net Sales                                      | ↓ = issue in price, cost or mix         | core profitability metric      |
| Gross Margin Amount      | Absolute margin contribution             | Net Sales − COGS                                                    | indicates economic value                | P&L steering                   |
| Price Realization %      | Execution of pricing strategy            | Net Price / List Price                                              | <100% = discounts or concessions        | pricing discipline             |
| Discount %               | Discount intensity                       | (List Price − Net Price) / List Price                               | high → risk of value leakage            | discount governance            |
| Margin vs Plan %         | Margin deviation to plan                 | (GM % − GM Plan %) / GM Plan %                                      | <0% = underperformance                   | forecasting & budget steering  |
| Margin Leakage Amount    | Quantified margin loss                   | (GM Target % − GM %) × Net Sales                                    | monetary view of leakage                | corrective actions             |

## 4. Business Logic & Thresholds
- GM % persistently below defined target = priority issue.
- Margin vs Plan % < −5 % on region/channel level = action required.
- Price Realization % < 95 % without promo justification = pricing review.
- Discount % high without volume uplift = discount optimization.
- Top-10 leakage contributors = mandatory deep-dive topics.

## 5. Action Codes

| Code | Name                   | Description                                       | Trigger                                | Expected Effect          |
|------|------------------------|---------------------------------------------------|-----------------------------------------|--------------------------|
| P1   | Price Review           | Adjust list/net prices                            | low GM %, negative margin vs plan       | GM recovery              |
| P2   | Discount Optimization  | Rationalize discounts and concessions             | high discount %, low realization        | lower margin leakage     |
| P3   | Price Architecture     | Redesign price logic (tiers, bundles, segments)   | inconsistent margins                     | structural improvement   |
| M1   | Mix Optimization       | Shift toward higher-margin products               | weak mix                                 | higher average GM %      |
| C1   | Cost Review            | Validate standard/actual cost accuracy            | GM % drop without price/mix movement     | stabilize profitability  |

## 6. 3–30–300 Page Layout

## 7. Dependencies & Constraints
- Accurate and stable COGS data required.
- Price List and Net Price must be available on invoice-line level.
- GM Plan % or GM Target % required for variance and leakage metrics.

## 8. Success Criteria
- Reduction of margin leakage in focus segments.
- Lower share of uncontrolled discounts.
- Improved consistency of GM % across regions/channels.
- Adoption rate >80 % in pricing and commercial reviews.
