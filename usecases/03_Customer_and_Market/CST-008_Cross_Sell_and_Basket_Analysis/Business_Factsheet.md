# Cross-Sell & Basket Analysis - Business Factsheet

## 1. Summary
- **Business Goal:** Increase average basket value and cross-sell between categories by identifying product affinities and targeting the right offers to the right customers and channels.

---
- **Target Audience:** Head of CRM / Category Management
- **Business Priority:** High
- **Expected Impact:** +5"10 % basket size and cross-sell ratio in target segments.

## 2. Core Questions
- What is the current average basket value and number of items per transaction?
- Which product categories are frequently bought together?
- Which customer segments have the highest cross-sell potential?
- How do campaigns or recommendations change basket metrics?
