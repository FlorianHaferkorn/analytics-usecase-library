# Innovation Revenue Contribution

## 1. Business Goal
Measure how much of total revenue and margin is generated by new and recently launched products, and assess whether innovation efforts translate into tangible commercial impact.

---

## 2. Business Context
Innovation portfolios often focus on idea counts and project milestones rather than commercial outcomes.  
Without a clear view on revenue and margin contribution from new products, it is hard to justify R&D spend and prioritize innovation projects.  
This Use Case links innovation metrics to sales performance and portfolio health.

---

## 3. Key Questions
- What share of total revenue comes from products launched in the last X years?
- How do new products perform vs. the rest of the portfolio in terms of margin and growth?
- Are there categories or regions where innovation underperforms?
- How does innovation contribution evolve over time compared to targets?

---

## 4. Key KPIs
| KPI                          | Definition                                      | Unit | Format   |
|------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------|------|----------|
| New Product Revenue Share %  | Revenue from new products / total revenue      | %    | 1 decimal |
| Innovation Rate %            | Share of portfolio that is considered new      | %    | 1 decimal |
| Net Sales Amount             | Total revenue                                  | EUR  | € #,0.00 |
| Gross Margin %               | (Net Sales – COGS) / Net Sales                 | %    | 1 decimal |

---

## 5. Required Attributes (Business-Level)
- Product with lifecycle stage and launch date
- Org (region/business unit)
- Net Sales Amount

---

## 6. Segmentation & Hierarchies
- Product: Category > Subcategory > SKU  
- Lifecycle: Stage (Launch / Growth / Mature / Decline)  
- Org: Region > Business Unit  
- Time: Year > Quarter > Month  

---

## 7. Scope & Assumptions
- Definition of “new product” (e.g., launched in last 24 months) must be clearly documented and applied consistently.
- Revenue is measured net of returns and discounts.

---

## 8. Data Freshness & Cadence
- Data refresh: monthly.
- Reporting cadence: monthly and quarterly portfolio reviews.

---

## 9. Edge Cases & QA Rules
- Products without launch date are flagged and excluded from new product KPIs.
- Lifecycle stages must be mutually exclusive and cover 100 % of active SKUs.

---

## 10. Minimum Viable Dataset (MVD)
- Required:
  - Sales fact with Net Sales Amount, ProductID, OrgID, Date.
  - Product dimension with LifecycleStage and LaunchDate.
- Optional:
  - Innovation project metadata to link SKUs to innovation programs.

---

## 11. Typical Actions
| Action                                     | Code | Expected Effect                     |
|--------------------------------------------|------|-------------------------------------|
| Reallocate budget to high-performing launches | I2 | Higher revenue contribution         |
| Refine stage-gate process for weak launches | SP1 | Higher success rate for innovations |
| Adjust assortments to phase out weak new SKUs | P2 | Portfolio freshness, stable margin  |

---

## 12. Expected Business Impact
| Dimension   | Expected Impact                  | Measurement     |
|-------------|----------------------------------|-----------------|
| Growth      | +3–5 % revenue from new products| vs prior year   |
| Profitability| Stable or +0.5 pp GM %         | vs prior year   |

---

## 13. Related Processes
Innovation Funnel → Product Development → Launch Management → Portfolio Review.

---

## 14. Insights & Learnings
Typical findings include a small share of launches driving most of innovation revenue, and categories with repeated underperforming launches.
