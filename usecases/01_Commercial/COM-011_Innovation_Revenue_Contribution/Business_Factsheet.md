# Innovation Revenue Contribution - Business Factsheet

## 1. Summary
- **Business Goal:** Measure how much of total revenue and margin is generated by new and recently launched products, and assess whether innovation efforts translate into tangible commercial impact.
- **Target Audience:** Head of Innovation / Head of Product
- **Business Priority:** High
- **Expected Impact:** Increase revenue share from new products and strengthen portfolio freshness.

## 2. Core Questions
- What share of total revenue comes from products launched in the last X years?
- How do new products perform vs. the rest of the portfolio in terms of margin and growth?
- Are there categories or regions where innovation underperforms?
- How does innovation contribution evolve over time compared to targets?

## 3. KPI Set (Business View)
| KPI                          | Definition                                      | Unit | Format   |
|------------------------------|-------------------------------------------------|------|----------|
| New Product Revenue Share %  | Revenue from new products / total revenue      | %    | 1 decimal |
| Innovation Rate %            | Share of portfolio that is considered new      | %    | 1 decimal |
| Net Sales Amount             | Total revenue                                  | EUR  |  #,0.00 |
| Gross Margin %               | (Net Sales - COGS) / Net Sales                 | %    | 1 decimal |

## 4. Business Logic & Thresholds
- Products without launch date are flagged and excluded from new product KPIs.
- Lifecycle stages must be mutually exclusive and cover 100 % of active SKUs.

## 5. Action Codes (Business Perspective)
| Action                                     | Code | Expected Effect                     |
|--------------------------------------------|------|-------------------------------------|
| Reallocate budget to high-performing launches | I2 | Higher revenue contribution         |
| Refine stage-gate process for weak launches | SP1 | Higher success rate for innovations |
| Adjust assortments to phase out weak new SKUs | P2 | Portfolio freshness, stable margin  |

## 6. 3-30-300 Page Layout

### 6.1 3-Second Layer (Insight)
- KPI cards for New Product Revenue Share %, Innovation Rate %, Net Sales Amount, Gross Margin % with Plan/LY deltas.
- Threshold coloring for immediate outliers.
- Short callout summarizing key variance.

### 6.2 30-Second Layer (Story)
- Trend chart (12-24M) for main KPIs.
- Variance bridge vs Plan/LY by driver.
- Ranking visuals for top/bottom segments.

### 6.3 300-Second Layer (Detail)
- Matrix/table with Org/Product/Customer drill-down.
- Drill-through to transactional detail (orders/invoices).
- Export-ready table including action status.

## 7. Dependencies & Constraints
- Definition of "new product" (e.g., launched in last 24 months) must be clearly documented and applied consistently.
- Revenue is measured net of returns and discounts.

## 8. Success Criteria
| Dimension   | Expected Impact                  | Measurement     |
|-------------|----------------------------------|-----------------|
| Growth      | +3-5 % revenue from new products| vs prior year   |
| Profitability| Stable or +0.5 pp GM %         | vs prior year   |
