# Action Codes (Operational Levers)

Action codes represent standardized levers that can be triggered based on analytical findings.  
They ensure consistency across domains and make automation possible (notifications, workflows, etc.).

---

## Structure
| Code | Category | Description | Expected Impact |
|------|-----------|--------------|-----------------|
| P1 | Pricing | Adjust list prices or reprice segments. | Revenue ↑, GM % stable or ↑. |
| P2 | Pricing | Tighten discount corridors, improve price discipline. | GM % ↑ 1–2 pp. |
| D1 | Promotion | Optimize promo calendar, shift low-ROI periods. | Forecast accuracy ↑, NS variance ↓. |
| D2 | Promotion | Reallocate marketing budget to high-performing campaigns. | ROI ↑, Churn ↓. |
| M3 | Mix / Channel | Rebalance channel mix toward high-margin segments. | Profitability ↑, volume stable. |
| PC2 | Procurement | Renegotiate supplier terms or rebid. | COGS ↓ 1–3 %. |
| PC4 | Procurement | Implement supplier scorecards linked to OTIF/quality. | Performance visibility ↑. |
| I1 | Inventory | Tune safety stock levels. | Capital ↓, service ↑. |
| I2 | Inventory | Allocate limited stock to key channels/regions. | Revenue stabilization. |
| W1 | Working Capital | Accelerate collections (e.g., factoring, dunning). | DSO ↓, cash ↑. |
| W2 | Working Capital | Extend supplier payment terms. | DPO ↑, liquidity ↑. |
| C1 | Customer | Execute save campaigns for at-risk customers. | Churn ↓, CLV ↑. |
| C4 | Customer | Cross-/Upsell initiatives. | CLV ↑, retention ↑. |
| SP1 | Strategy | Invest/divest in business units or categories. | ROI optimization. |
| O2 | Operations | Efficiency or automation projects. | Opex ↓, output ↑. |
