# Strategic Overview Page Template (T1)

## Purpose
High-level KPI view for executives. Focus on trend stability, strategic variances, and portfolio/resource allocation signals. 3–30–300 ready.

## Visual Structure (Slots)

### 3-Second Layer (fixed positions)
- Slot A – KPI Card 1
- Slot B – KPI Card 2
- Slot C – KPI Card 3
- Slot D – KPI Card 4

### 30-Second Layer (structured, slot-driven)
- Slot E – Trend OR Variance OR Mix
- Slot F – Ranking OR Mix Breakdown
- Slot G – Detail Insight (optional)

### 300-Second Layer (diagnostic)
- Slot H – Matrix / Drill Table
- Slot I – Action Code Panel

## Visual Standards (Whitelist)
- KPI Cards
- Line (12–24M)
- Waterfall
- Horizontal Bar
- 100% Stacked Bar
- Small Multiples
- Matrix

Forbidden: Pie, Donut, Combo, Area, Decomposition Tree, Gauge, Custom visuals.

## Slicers (max 3)
- Time
- Org
- Segment
