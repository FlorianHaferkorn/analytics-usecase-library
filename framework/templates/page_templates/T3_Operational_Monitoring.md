# Operational Monitoring Template (T3)

## Purpose
Daily/weekly execution control. Highlight exceptions, SLA breaches, volume flow, stockouts, delays, and corrective actions.

## Visual Structure (Slots)

### 3-Second Layer
- Slot A – KPI 1
- Slot B – KPI 2
- Slot C – KPI 3

### 30-Second Layer
- Slot D – Daily/Weekly Trend
- Slot E – Exception Table (mandatory)
- Slot F – Top/Bottom Analysis

### 300-Second Layer
- Slot G – Drill Matrix OR Small Multiples
- Slot H – Operational Action Panel

## Visual Standards
Allowed: KPI Cards, Column, Line, Horizontal Bar, Matrix, Small Multiples, Exception Table  
Forbidden: Pie, Donut, Area, Combo, Maps, Decomposition Tree.

## Slicers
- Time
- Org
- Optional segmentation
