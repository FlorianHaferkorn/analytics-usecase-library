# Tactical Variance & Drivers Template (T2)

## Purpose
Diagnosis of performance gaps. Focus on variances, driver structures, mix effects, and tactical decision support.

## Visual Structure (Slots)

### 3-Second Layer
- Slot A – Core KPI
- Slot B – Δ vs Plan
- Slot C – Δ vs LY
- Slot D – Forecast Accuracy OR Rate KPI

### 30-Second Layer
- Slot E – Variance Bridge (mandatory)
- Slot F – Ranking (mandatory)
- Slot G – Trend OR Mix OR Attribution (optional)

### 300-Second Layer
- Slot H – Matrix (mandatory)
- Slot I – Tactical Action Code Panel

## Visual Standards
Allowed: KPI Cards, Waterfall, Horizontal Bar, Line, 100% Bar, Small Multiples, Matrix  
Forbidden: Pie, Donut, Combo, Funnel, Scatter (non-prescriptive), Gauge.

- Time
- Org
