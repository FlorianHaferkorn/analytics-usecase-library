# Technical Glossary

Purpose:
- Standardize technical terms used in the ActionReady Analytics Framework (models, data, operations).

Core Terms:
- Semantic model: Curated layer with governed measures, relationships, and display folders.
- Data contract: Agreement defining facts/dims, grain, keys, RI/QA rules, and SLAs.
- KPI ID: Stable identifier (dot-notation) used across catalogs, models, and reports.
- Action code: Coded remediation linked to KPI deviations (see action codes portfolio).
- RI/QA asserts: Referential integrity and quality checks enforced on datasets.
- 3-30-300: UX principle (3s headline, 30s story, 300s deep dive).

Usage:
- Keep definitions short, consistent, and cross-linked with business glossary and KPI catalog.
- Add new terms only when reused across domains; avoid duplicates.
- Review with governance when terms change or are deprecated.
