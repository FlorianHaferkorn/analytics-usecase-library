# How to Use Action Codes

Purpose:
- Apply the Action Codes Portfolio in reports, alerts, and operational playbooks.

Steps:
- Map KPI deviations to one or more action codes based on the portfolio.
- Surface the recommended actions alongside the KPI in reports (use templates).
- Track execution/ownership of actions; feed back results to refine mappings.

Guidelines:
- Use only the IDs and names from `ActionCodes_Portfolio.md`.
- Keep mappings in `usecases/UseCase_ActionCode_Map.yaml` and rationale aligned.
- Review mappings when KPIs, thresholds, or business rules change.
