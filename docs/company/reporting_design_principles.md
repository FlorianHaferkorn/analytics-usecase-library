# Reporting Design Principles

Purpose:
- Ensure ActionReady Analytics Framework reports are consistent, decision-focused, and intuitive.

Principles:
- Audience-first: start with the key question and decision per role.
- 3-30-300: headline in 3s, storyline in 30s, deep-dive in 300s.
- Single narrative: one primary KPI with clear supporting evidence and actions.
- Consistent language: use canonical KPI names, action codes, and glossary terms.
- Accessibility and clarity: minimal chrome, high contrast, no unnecessary decoration.

Application:
- Use page templates (overview/insights/explorer) to enforce structure.
- Keep visuals limited and purposeful; prefer comparisons and trends over raw tables.
- Surface recommended actions next to the KPI deviation, linked to action codes.
