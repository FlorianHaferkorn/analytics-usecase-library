# Business Strategy

Purpose:
- Anchor the ActionReady Analytics Framework to the company’s goals, domains, and decision questions.

Strategic Anchors:
- Growth & Margin: price/volume/mix, promotion effectiveness, channel mix.
- Efficiency & Liquidity: OTIF/CCC, inventory efficiency, procurement variance.
- Customer & Market: churn/retention, acquisition funnel, segment profitability.
- Workforce & Innovation: productivity, turnover, innovation rate.
- ESG & Governance: emission/energy targets, compliance, data quality.

Implications for analytics:
- Use cases must tie to strategic KPIs and action codes; prioritize 3-30-300 storytelling.
- Enforce KPI IDs, action codes, and domain ownership as single sources of truth.
- Keep cadence and distribution aligned to decision cycles (who sees what, how often).

Next Steps:
- Confirm domain ownership and KPIs with business owners.
- Prioritize the next wave of use cases per domain.
- Align distribution architecture and UX templates with the strategy above.
