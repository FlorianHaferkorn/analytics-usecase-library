# Domains

Purpose:
- Define the business domains covered by the ActionReady Analytics Framework and their ownership.

Domains (current scope):
- Commercial / Sales
- Operational Efficiency / Supply Chain
- Customer & Market
- Corporate & Strategy (incl. Finance/Workforce)
- ESG
- Governance
- Innovation & People / HR

Notes:
- Each domain maps to use cases, KPIs, semantic models, and data contracts.
- Domain owners are accountable for definitions, KPIs, and action code applicability.
