# Key Questions

Purpose:
- Capture the recurring executive questions the ActionReady Analytics Framework must answer.

Examples (per domain):
- Commercial: Are revenue/margin gaps driven by price, volume, or mix? Which actions close them fastest?
- Operations: Where are we leaking OTIF/CCC performance, and what’s the root cause?
- Customer & Market: Which segments are at churn risk, and which actions retain them?
- Corporate & Strategy: Are strategic initiatives on track (ROI, schedule, benefit realization)?
- ESG: Are we meeting emission/energy targets, and where do we deviate?
- Governance: Are controls, access, and data quality within tolerance?
- Innovation & People: Welche Investments in Innovation/Skills liefern messbaren Beitrag?

Use:
- Align use case scoping and KPI/action selection to these questions.
- Prioritize backlog items that answer the most critical questions.
